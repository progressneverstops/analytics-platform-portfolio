# Experimentation Case Study

## Problem Statement
Activation in week one is flat. Product wants to test a shorter onboarding flow.

## Hypothesis
Reducing onboarding steps increases week-one activation without reducing revenue.

## Dataset
Synthetic dataset in `data/experiment_events.csv` with exposure and conversion outcomes.

## Analysis
See `analysis/analysis.sql` and `analysis/summary.md`.

## Recommendation
Roll out the shorter onboarding flow to 100% of new users, with guardrail monitoring.
